### 🚀 About Me

I am an **AI Engineer** with hands-on experience in **Computer Vision, Edge AI, Document Intelligence (OCR), and Generative AI (LLMs, RAG, and Speech AI)**. My core engineering philosophy focuses on delivering **lightweight, efficient, and production-ready AI systems** — from optimizing deep neural networks for resource-constrained edge hardware (NVIDIA Jetson, Rockchip NPU) to orchestrating scalable LLM agent pipelines and full-stack AI services.

- 🧠 **Edge AI & Model Compression**: Proven track record reducing model parameters by **92.6%** via Knowledge Distillation and deploying quantized models (FP16/INT8) on **NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX** and **Rockchip RK3566 NPU** via TensorRT and RKNN.
- 👁️ **Computer Vision & Document AI**: Architected end-to-end OCR and key-value extraction for complex legal/administrative documents, and deployed real-time super-resolution systems for live traffic video streaming.
- 🗣️ **Generative AI & Speech Systems**: Engineered end-to-end voice-enabled RAG pipelines combining Whisper ASR, local CPU LLM inference (`llama.cpp`), vector retrieval (`Qdrant`), and XTTS.
- 🛠️ **Production & Full-Stack MLOps**: Solid engineering foundation across **FastAPI, Docker, Linux/Nginx, Supabase, React, and TypeScript**, with automated workflow orchestration using **n8n**.
